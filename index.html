Grandma's Little Cookies, established in 2023 by a group of teenagers who met at Henderson High School, comprises Olly, William, Connor, and Leslie. The group has been performing at cafes, parties, corporate events, and weddings. Our signature blend of Jazz, Pop, and Rock creates an atmosphere of pure joy. This helps us in our mission to spread joy through music, connect with our audience, and create memorable experiences that resonate long after the last note is played.
